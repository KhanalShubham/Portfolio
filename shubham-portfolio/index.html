C:\SHUBHAM\about_me.txt

I build systems that bridge human creativity, hardware interactions, and machine intelligence. My work spans from training computer-vision pipelines (OpenCV, YOLOv8, Pose Estimation) for grassroots tennis analytics, to wiring hardware steering wheels with Raspberry Pi PWM signals, to architecting production full-stack platforms like Hope Care (JWT, Socket.io, Khalti payment, Docker) and Medical Exchange Nepal.

Alongside engineering, athletics has defined my discipline: representing Nepal at the Under-15 Junior World Soft Tennis Championships in Korea and winning Bronze Medals at the 7th and 8th National Games of Nepal.

As a Teaching Assistant at Softwarica College, I guide aspiring software engineers in core programming and system architecture. I believe software should have soul — crafted with care down to the smallest micro-interaction.
